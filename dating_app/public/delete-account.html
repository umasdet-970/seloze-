Delete your Seloze account

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Delete it in the app

This is the fastest way, and you don't need to contact us.

  1. Open Seloze and go to Profile, then tap the settings (gear) icon.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm it's you: enter your password (email accounts) or confirm with Google (Google accounts).
  4. Tap Delete. You'll be signed out and the account is permanently removed.

What gets deleted

Your login, your profile (name, bio, interests, location and preferences), your uploaded photos, your likes, matches, blocks and notifications, and your entries in other members' match lists.

What we may keep

Where needed for safety, fraud prevention or legal compliance, we may retain limited records, such as reports made about an account. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can't open the app?

Email umamaheswar.sdet@gmail.com from the address registered on your account, with the subject "Seloze account deletion request", and we'll delete your account for you.
