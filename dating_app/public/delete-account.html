Delete your Seloze-Global Dating App account

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Request deletion

You can ask us to delete your Seloze-Global Dating App account and data without opening the app. Send us a deletion request by email:

Request account deletion by email

Please write from the email address you use with Seloze, or include the email address or phone number you signed up with and how you sign in (email and password, Google, or phone), so we can confirm the account is yours. We may reply to check it is you before we delete anything. We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

Or delete it in the app

This is the fastest way. Deleting in the app takes effect straight away.

  1. Open Seloze and go to Profile, then tap the settings (gear) icon.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm it's you: enter your password (email accounts) or confirm with Google (Google accounts). Phone sign-in accounts may be asked to sign out and back in first.
  4. Tap Delete. You'll be signed out and the account is permanently removed.

What gets deleted

Your login, your profile (name, date of birth, gender, bio, interests, location and preferences), your photos, your likes, matches, blocks and notifications, and your conversations.

What we may keep

Where needed for safety, fraud prevention or legal compliance, we may keep limited records, such as reports made about an account. Usage analytics processed by Google are kept under Google's own retention settings. See our Privacy Policy for details.
